## Practical assessment — The Westhaven Training Centre Announcement
**20 minutes • 10 questions • 100 points • Pass 80**

> This is an entirely fictional controlled investigation. The institutions, records, events and dates are teaching examples and cannot be retrieved from the real internet.

### Assignment
**What public information supports the claim that Westhaven Training Centre received official approval to launch a new evening course, and had begun delivering it by 30 September 2026?**

Demonstrate query design, official-source selection, source tracing and documentation. Use the supplied packet, not real-world searches for these fictional entities.

### Evidence packet

#### Document A — Training centre announcement
*Fictional document • Published 2 September 2026*

Westhaven states that it has applied for approval to run an evening course. It expects to start classes in October.

#### Document B — Education committee minutes
*Fictional document • Published 9 September 2026*

The committee recommends approval but says a final authorization decision is required.

#### Document C — Local news report
*Fictional document • Published 12 September 2026*

Headline: 'Westhaven Course Approved.' The article cites only Document B.

#### Document D — Community website article
*Fictional document • Published 14 September 2026*

Repeats Document C's approval claim and names Document C as its source.

#### Document E — Official authorization register
*Fictional document • Published 22 September 2026*

The responsible authority publishes a final authorization dated 19 September. It confirms permission for the course, not delivery of classes.

#### Document F — Official course timetable
*Fictional document • Published 28 September 2026*

Westhaven announces that its first evening class is scheduled for 7 October 2026. No record of an earlier class is supplied.
