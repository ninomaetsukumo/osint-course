## M01-PA — The Northbridge Library Announcement
**Replacement practical assessment approved 13 October 2026 | 20 minutes | 10 questions | 100 points | Pass at 80**

> This case and its documents are fictional instructional material. They are newly authored replacements for an unrecovered assessment. They are not representations of real events.

### Assignment
A fictional town called Northbridge is planning a new public library. Several sources make different statements about the project. Determine whether the library was approved, opened, and operating by **30 September 2026**. Use only the supplied documents. Do not search for real people or organizations.

### Evidence packet
#### Document A — Council meeting minutes
*Fictional council record, 10 September 2026*

The council voted to recommend the library project for approval. The final authorization was still pending.

#### Document B — Local newspaper
*Fictional news article, 12 September 2026*

The headline states that the library has been approved. The article relies only on Document A.

#### Document C — Final council authorization
*Fictional official decision, 18 September 2026*

Formal approval is recorded for the new library project. The authorization does not confirm that the library is open.

#### Document D — Library announcement
*Fictional library website, 25 September 2026*

The library announces that visitors are expected to be welcomed beginning 5 October 2026.

#### Document E — Community forum post
*Fictional anonymous post, 29 September 2026*

An anonymous poster claims that the library is already open. No photographs, visitor records, or independently verifiable supporting information are provided.

### Assessment questions
Each question is worth 10 points. Select one best answer.

### Question 1
What is the main research question?
A. Who designed the library?
B. Was the library approved, opened, and operating by 30 September?
C. How many books will the library own?

### Question 2

C. The library was formally opened

### Question 3

C. Document E

### Question 4

### Question 5

B. The library announced an expected opening date
C. The library operated throughout September

### Question 6

C. An official operational record

### Question 7

### Question 8

### Question 9

### Question 10
