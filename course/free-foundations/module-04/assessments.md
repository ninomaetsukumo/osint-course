## Practical assessment — The Harborview Clinic Opening Claim
**20 minutes • 10 questions • 100 points • Passing threshold 80**

**All case names, documents, dates and activities are fictional.** Do not use real patient records or contact real clinics.

### Investigation question
Was Harborview Clinic formally authorized and providing patient services by 30 September 2026?

### Evidence packet
#### Document A — Official licensing committee minutes
*Fictional source • Published 10 September 2026*

The committee recommends licensing approval. A final decision by the responsible authority is still required.

#### Document B — Local news article
*Fictional source • Published 12 September 2026*

The article claims the clinic has opened. It cites only Document A and provides no evidence of patient services.

#### Document C — Community news page
*Fictional source • Published 14 September 2026*

The page repeats Document B and identifies it as the sole source.

#### Document D — Final licensing decision register
*Fictional source • Published 20 September 2026*

The responsible authority records formal licensing approval dated 18 September, subject to the stated conditions. It does not confirm actual service delivery.

#### Document E — Official clinic announcement
*Fictional source • Published 27 September 2026*

The clinic states that its first patient appointments are scheduled from 5 October 2026.

#### Document F — Anonymous online allegation
*Fictional source • Published 29 September 2026*

An unidentified commenter alleges that patients were secretly treated in September. No verifiable records or independently supported details accompany the allegation.
