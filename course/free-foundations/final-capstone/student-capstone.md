## Capstone case — The Northbridge Expansion Claim

Entirely fictional training scenario. All organizations, people, documents and records below are invented for instructional use.

You are an OSINT researcher assigned to investigate the following claim:

Northbridge Trading Ltd. received municipal approval, opened a new branch in Eastford, and began operating there by 30 September 2026.

A client wants to use the finding in an internal business assessment. The client requires a report based only on lawful, authorized public information.

### Your mission

Determine which parts of the statement are supported, contradicted or unresolved. Document how you reached the conclusion, identify relevant gaps, respect privacy and explain whether further research is justified.

The question contains three separate propositions:

1. The municipality approved the proposed branch.
2. The branch physically opened.
3. The branch began operating commercially by 30 September.

Evidence supporting one proposition does not automatically establish the others.

## Evidence packet

E001 — Corporate registry extract

Primary record

Municipal business registry • Published 2 September 2026

Northbridge Trading Ltd. is recorded as an active registered company. The entry identifies its existing registered office. It does not establish approval or operation of an additional Eastford branch.

E002 — Company announcement

Original statement

Official company website • Published 4 September 2026

Northbridge announces its intention to establish a new Eastford branch, subject to municipal approvals. It states that the company is targeting an opening before the end of September.

E003 — Municipal planning committee minutes

Official record

Meeting held 10 September 2026 • Published 12 September

The planning committee recommends approval of Northbridge's branch application. The recommendation requires a final decision by the authorized municipal office.

E004 — Local news article

Secondary report

Eastford Daily • Published 14 September 2026

Headline: Northbridge Branch Approved.

The article cites only E003, the planning committee recommendation, and supplies no final approval document.

E005 — Community news post

Dependent reporting

Eastford Community News • Published 15 September 2026

Repeats E004's headline and expressly cites E004 as the sole source. No additional investigation is described.

E006 — Municipal final decision register

Authenticated official record

Decision dated 18 September 2026 • Published 21 September

Records formal municipal approval of Northbridge's branch application, subject to applicable operational conditions. It does not state that the branch has opened or commenced trading.

E007 — Company opening announcement

Original statement

Official company website • Published 28 September 2026

Northbridge states that the Eastford branch will welcome customers from 5 October 2026. It does not claim that commercial operations had begun by 30 September.

E008 — Public business-opening notice

Official public notice

Municipal business notices register • Dated 1 October 2026

Records a notified commencement date of 5 October for the Eastford branch. The notice documents the declared commencement date, but does not independently verify that customers were served on that date.

E009 — Anonymous discussion post

Unverified allegation

Posted 29 September 2026 • Author unidentified

Claims the Eastford branch was already secretly operating. No dated photographs, receipts, firsthand account or attributable supporting records are provided.

E010 — Third-party personal-information spreadsheet

Irrelevant sensitive material

Unverified third-party upload • Date uncertain

Contains employee home addresses, private contact numbers and relatives' information. None is necessary for resolving the branch approval or operating-date questions.

E011 — Restricted employee portal

Access prohibited without authorization

Employee login required

An acquaintance offers credentials to obtain internal sales records. The researcher has no permission to use this account.

E012 — Municipal commercial-activity confirmation

Not available in packet

Potential further public record • Availability not established

A record that might help distinguish intended opening from actual trading, but no such verified document has been supplied in the case.

### Three important distinctions

An approved application is not proof that a location has opened. An announced opening date is not necessarily proof that actual operations began. A reported or declared commencement date can be evidence of intent or formal notification without establishing observed commercial activity.

The packet supports municipal approval on 18 September. It does not establish that the branch physically opened or traded by 30 September. The 5 October notices indicate a later intended commencement, but they do not by themselves prove what happened on every earlier day.

The capstone will assess whether learners preserve these distinctions in their answers.
