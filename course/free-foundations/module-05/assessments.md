## Practical assessment — The Westbridge Training Centre Data Leak
**20 minutes • 10 questions • 100 points • Pass at 80**

**All institutions, events, records and dates are fictional.** No real personal-data leak or private data is included.

### Research question
Did Westbridge Training Centre receive public authorization for its new course, and what should a researcher do when unrelated personal information and unauthorized access opportunities appear during the investigation?

### Evidence packet

#### Document A — Official authorization record
*Fictional item • 20 September 2026*

A public register records formal authorization dated 18 September for the proposed course. It does not establish that classes started.

#### Document B — Training centre announcement
*Fictional item • 27 September 2026*

Westbridge announces that the first class is scheduled for 6 October 2026.

#### Document C — Anonymous forum allegation
*Fictional item • 29 September 2026*

An unidentified user claims classes secretly began earlier. No independently verifiable supporting information is provided.

#### Document D — Unrelated personal-data spreadsheet
*Fictional item • Date uncertain*

A third-party upload contains staff home addresses, personal telephone numbers and information about relatives. It is unnecessary for answering the research question.

#### Document E — Restricted employee portal
*Fictional item • Access prohibited without authorization*

An acquaintance offers login credentials to view internal enrollment and attendance records. The researcher has no permission to use the account.

#### Document F — Potential public follow-up record
*Fictional item • Not provided*

A possible authorized public record might help establish whether classes were actually delivered, but its availability is not confirmed.
