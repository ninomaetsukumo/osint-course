## Practical assessment — The Eastford Community Learning Center

**Approved fictional case • 20 minutes • 100 points • Pass at 80**

All organizations, records, events and dates in this case are fictional and are included solely for instruction.

### Assignment
A client asks: **Was the Eastford Community Learning Center officially approved and open for public classes by 30 September 2026?**

Create a research question, define the scope, plan the investigation, record the evidence and reach an appropriately limited conclusion.

### Evidence packet

#### Document A — Community proposal
*Fictional source • Published 2 September 2026*

The center's official public page announces a proposal to open a new learning facility, subject to approval.

#### Document B — Education committee minutes
*Fictional source • Published 10 September 2026*

The committee recommends approval. The minutes state that a final decision is required.

#### Document C — Final authorization register
*Fictional source • Published 20 September 2026*

An official record lists formal approval dated 18 September. It does not confirm that classes have started.

#### Document D — Opening announcement
*Fictional source • Published 27 September 2026*

The center states that its first public classes are scheduled for 6 October 2026.

#### Document E — Anonymous discussion comment
*Fictional source • Published 29 September 2026*

A commenter claims classes have secretly started, but supplies no independently verifiable evidence.

### Assessment tasks and scoring
| Task | Required work | Maximum points |
